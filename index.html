The Firm

A practice built on diligence, discretion and results.

AIC Solicitors serves a broad clientele of corporations, entrepreneurs, financial institutions, families and property investors. Our work joins two disciplines that rarely sit in one firm: rigorous legal practice and deep, practical knowledge of the Nigerian property market.

From incorporation and commercial agreements to land acquisition, title perfection, estate administration and courtroom advocacy, the firm offers clients a single trusted point of counsel for both their enterprises and their assets.

We hold a simple conviction: a client's matter deserves the principal's attention. Every brief in this firm is handled with the seniority, candour and urgency it would receive if it were our own.
